# Trailer video

Drop your teaser video here as `trailer.mp4` (referenced from `lib/content.ts`).

Tips for a smooth mobile trailer:
- Vertical/portrait 9:16, under ~15MB — this plays inline, muted, and loops when scrolled into view.
- H.264 MP4 keeps compatibility broadest across mobile browsers.
- Until you add a file, the trailer section shows the poster image instead.
